# CrackGate — Clean Zero-State Initialization (No Pre-Loaded Demo Data)

Remove all default pre-seeded study logs, YouTube lectures, mock test attempts, and static curated questions so **CrackGate** starts as a clean, personal preparation workspace with official GATE syllabus subjects and chapters initialized at `0%` progress.

## User Review & Critical Decisions

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Confirmed from your preferences:
> - **Official Syllabus Retained at 0% Progress**: All 6 GATE streams (`CS`, `DA`, `EC`, `EE`, `ME`, `CE`) retain their official subjects and chapter lists, with every topic reset to `0h` studied and `Not Started` status.
> - **Zero Pre-Loaded Logs, Lectures, Test History, or Curated Questions**: `studyLogs`, `lectures`, `testAttempts`, and `questions` initialize as empty arrays (`[]`).
> - **Mock Test Arena Streamlined for AI & PDF Chapter Tests**: The Mock Test configurator defaults directly to **AI Custom Mock Generator** and **Upload PDF Mock** (plus any questions you generate or extract from your own PDFs).

---

## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Eliminates all pre-populated sample data across the application and resets browser storage to a fresh `v2` store (`crackgate_state_v2`) so every metric, chart, lecture playlist, and test score reflects only what the student personally adds or completes.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: GATE aspirants who want an authentic, unpolluted personal tracker starting from day one of their own preparation.
- **Key Value**: Zero dummy noise—every hour logged, every YouTube lecture pasted, and every mock test score is 100% genuine student telemetry.

---

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

- **Key User Flows**:
  1. **Overview & GATE DA Radar at Zero State**: On first load, Syllabus Coverage reads `0%`, Total Logged Hours reads `0 / <Target>h`, YouTube Lecture Progress reads `0 / 0`, and the GATE DA Radar Chart displays a clean `0%` baseline polygon ready to expand as topics are studied and tested.
  2. **Daily Progress Logger**: All syllabus chapters across `CS`, `DA`, `EC`, `EE`, `ME`, and `CE` start as `Not Started (0h studied)`, with an empty study session history ready for the student's first entry.
  3. **Mock Test Arena (AI & PDF Driven)**: Defaults to **AI Generator** and **Upload PDF Mock**. Any questions generated by AI or parsed from an uploaded PDF are saved to the student's personal question bank for future practice.
  4. **YouTube Lecture Studio**: Starts with a clean empty state prompting the student to paste their first YouTube lecture URL for instant auto-fetch and save.
- **Visual Identity & Theme**:
  - *Aesthetic Direction*: High-Craft Light Mode engineering workspace (`#F8FAFC` canvas, `#FFFFFF` cards, `#E2E8F0` borders, `#0F172A` slate typography, `#2563EB` Cobalt Blue primary actions).
  - *Empty States*: Crisp, actionable zero-states in every view guiding the student to log their first study session, generate or upload a PDF mock test, or paste a YouTube video link.

---

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Versioned Local Storage Reset (`crackgate_state_v2`)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Switch the persistence key to `crackgate_state_v2` and remove legacy seed merging for `questions`, `lectures`, and `studyLogs`.
  - *Why*: Guarantees that any existing demo data previously cached in the browser's `localStorage` is immediately cleared without requiring manual browser cache clearing.
- **Decision 2: Streamlining Mock Test Modes**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Focus the Mock Test configurator on **AI Custom Mock Generator** and **Upload PDF Mock** (while allowing re-drilling of questions the student has previously generated or uploaded).
  - *Why*: Directly matches the confirmed choice to clear all static curated questions and rely on AI and uploaded PDF mock tests.
